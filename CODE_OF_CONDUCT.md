# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.
Discuss ideas and results critically, but treat people with patience and
professionalism.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
publishing private information and sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit,
remove or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participants
whose behaviour harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through a GitHub
security advisory or another private contact channel published by the
maintainers. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
